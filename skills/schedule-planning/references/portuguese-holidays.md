# Public holidays in Portugal, 2026 and 2027

National holidays close most studios; the municipal ones close some. Carnival Tuesday is not a legal
holiday but many studios treat it as one. Always ask before assuming a studio closes on a date.

| Holiday | 2026 | 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Ano Novo (New Year's Day) | Thu 1 Jan | Fri 1 Jan |
| Carnaval (Carnival Tuesday, optional) | Tue 17 Feb | Tue 9 Feb |
| Sexta-feira Santa (Good Friday) | Fri 3 Apr | Fri 26 Mar |
| Páscoa (Easter Sunday) | Sun 5 Apr | Sun 28 Mar |
| Dia da Liberdade | Sat 25 Apr | Sun 25 Apr |
| Dia do Trabalhador | Fri 1 May | Sat 1 May |
| Corpo de Deus (Corpus Christi) | Thu 4 Jun | Thu 27 May |
| Dia de Portugal | Wed 10 Jun | Thu 10 Jun |
| Assunção de Nossa Senhora | Sat 15 Aug | Sun 15 Aug |
| Implantação da República | Mon 5 Oct | Tue 5 Oct |
| Todos os Santos | Sun 1 Nov | Mon 1 Nov |
| Restauração da Independência | Tue 1 Dec | Wed 1 Dec |
| Imaculada Conceição | Tue 8 Dec | Wed 8 Dec |
| Natal (Christmas Day) | Fri 25 Dec | Sat 25 Dec |

Municipal holidays that come up often: Lisbon, Santo António, 13 June; Porto, São João, 24 June; Braga,
São João, 24 June; Coimbra, Rainha Santa Isabel, 4 July; Faro, 7 September.

Many studios also pause between Christmas and New Year, and run a reduced timetable in August. Neither is a
holiday: ask for the exact dates.
